🚨 Emergency Help & Safety Guide

Stay calm, stay informed and follow the appropriate safety measures during an emergency.

🌊 Flood

What to do:

  • Move to higher ground immediately.
  • Listen to official emergency instructions.
  • Keep important documents and emergency supplies with you.
  • Switch off electricity if it is safe to do so.
  • Avoid walking or driving through flood water.

Remember:

Do not enter moving flood water. It may be deeper and stronger than it appears.

🔥 Fire

What to do:

  • Stay calm and leave the building immediately.
  • Use stairs instead of elevators.
  • Stay low if there is smoke.
  • Never go back inside a burning building.
  • Call the fire service as soon as possible.

Remember:

If your clothes catch fire, remember: Stop, Drop and Roll.

🌍 Earthquake

What to do:

  • Drop to the ground.
  • Take cover under a strong table or desk.
  • Hold on until the shaking stops.
  • Stay away from windows and heavy objects.
  • After the shaking stops, move carefully to a safe area.

Remember:

Do not use elevators during or immediately after an earthquake.

🌀 Cyclone

What to do:

  • Stay indoors and away from windows.
  • Secure doors and windows.
  • Keep an emergency kit ready.
  • Follow official weather and evacuation warnings.
  • Do not go outside during the storm.

Remember:

Keep your phone charged and stay updated through official emergency announcements.
