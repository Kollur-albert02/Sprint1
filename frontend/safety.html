🚨 Emergency Safety Tips

Learn how to protect yourself, your family and your community before, during and after a disaster.

Be Prepared. Stay Safe.

Disasters can happen with little warning. Knowing what to do before an emergency occurs can help reduce risks and protect lives. Follow reliable instructions from emergency authorities and always prioritize your safety.

🌍

Earthquake Safety

During an earthquake, protect yourself from falling objects and remain calm.

  • Drop, cover and hold on.
  • Stay away from windows and glass.
  • Move away from objects that may fall.
  • If outdoors, stay away from buildings and power lines.
  • Be prepared for possible aftershocks.
🌊

Flood Safety

Floodwater can be dangerous and may hide hazards such as debris and damaged roads.

  • Move to higher ground when advised.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.
  • Stay informed about flood warnings.
  • Keep drinking water and food supplies ready.
  • Follow evacuation instructions from authorities.
🔥

Fire Safety

Fires can spread quickly. Leave dangerous areas immediately and follow emergency instructions.

  • Alert people nearby when a fire is detected.
  • Leave using the safest available exit.
  • Never use an elevator during a building fire.
  • Stay low if there is smoke.
  • Never return to a burning building.
🌀

Cyclone Safety

Strong winds and heavy rainfall during cyclones can damage buildings and infrastructure.

  • Monitor official weather warnings.
  • Stay indoors in a secure location.
  • Keep emergency supplies ready.
  • Stay away from windows.
  • Evacuate if instructed by authorities.

🎒 What To Do Before a Disaster

Preparation is one of the most important ways to reduce the impact of a disaster.

  • Prepare an emergency kit.
  • Store drinking water and non-perishable food.
  • Keep a flashlight and spare batteries.
  • Keep important documents protected.
  • Save emergency contact numbers.
  • Know the safest evacuation routes in your area.
  • Discuss an emergency plan with your family.

✅ Emergency Checklist

💧 Drinking water
🥫 Non-perishable food
🩹 First-aid supplies
🔦 Flashlight and batteries
📻 Battery-powered radio
🔋 Power bank
📄 Important documents
📱 Emergency contact information
📢 Whistle

🚨 During an Emergency

Stay calm, move away from immediate danger, and follow instructions from emergency services and local authorities. Do not spread unverified information or put yourself at additional risk while trying to help others.
