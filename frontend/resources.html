🎒 Disaster Resources

Essential resources and supplies that can help you stay prepared before, during and after a disaster.

💧

Drinking Water

Keep a sufficient supply of clean drinking water available during emergencies.

🥫

Emergency Food

Store non-perishable food that can be safely consumed during an emergency.

🩹

First Aid Kit

Keep basic first-aid supplies ready for minor injuries and emergencies.

🔦

Flashlight

Keep a flashlight and spare batteries ready in case of power failure.

📻

Emergency Radio

A battery-powered radio can help you receive important emergency updates.

📄

Important Documents

Keep copies of important documents protected and easily accessible.

🎒 Build Your Emergency Kit

Prepare an emergency bag before a disaster occurs. Keep it somewhere that is easy to access and make sure your family members know where it is.

  • Drinking water
  • Non-perishable food
  • First-aid supplies
  • Flashlight and extra batteries
  • Battery-powered radio
  • Power bank
  • Basic medicines and personal essentials
  • Copies of important documents
  • Whistle
  • Emergency contact information
