⚠️ Important During an Emergency

  • Stay calm and assess the situation before acting.
  • Move to a safe location if evacuation is advised.
  • Keep your phone charged and maintain communication.
  • Give emergency responders your exact location.
  • Do not spread unverified information or rumours.
  • Follow instructions from local authorities.

📍 Share Your Location

When contacting emergency services, provide your exact location, nearby landmarks and the nature of the emergency.
